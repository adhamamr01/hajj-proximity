## Hajj Proximity (free) — `com.hajjproximity.app`

### English (en-US)

**Title**
```
Hajj Proximity
```

**Short description**
```
Meeqat and Haram alerts, maps and an Ihram checklist for Hajj and Umrah.
```

**Full description**
```
Hajj Proximity helps pilgrims prepare for Ihram and know where they are in relation to the Meeqat and the Haram. It works in English and Arabic.

ALERTS AS YOU TRAVEL
• Get a notification as you approach one of the five Meeqat points, so you can prepare your Ihram in time.
• Get a notification when you enter or leave the Haram boundary around Makkah.
• Choose how early the Meeqat alert arrives: 10, 20 or 50 km.
• Turn each type of alert on or off.

MAPS
• See the five Meeqat points (Dhul-Hulayfah, Al-Juhfah, Qarn al-Manazil, Dhat 'Irq and Yalamlam) with who each one is for and its distance from Makkah.
• Follow the Meeqat boundary around Makkah, plus a reference circle at two stages (about 82.5 km) for those whose route does not pass a Meeqat.
• See the Haram boundary and whether you are inside it right now.
• Switch between map and satellite view.

IHRAM CHECKLIST
• A step-by-step checklist for entering Ihram: removing hair and trimming nails, ghusl, perfume, putting on the Ihram garments, the two rak'ahs, the intention, the full Talbiyah, and the acts prohibited in Ihram.
• Tick steps off as you go. Your progress is saved on your phone.

PRIVACY
• No account and no ads.
• Your location is used on your device only, to work out these alerts. It is not stored, shared or sent anywhere.
• The map needs an internet connection to load.

BACKGROUND LOCATION
If you turn on tracking, the app uses your location even when it is closed or not in use, so it can send the alerts above. You can turn tracking off at any time in Settings.

WANT THE FIDYAH CALCULATOR?
Hajj Proximity Premium adds a Fidyah calculator for Hajj and Umrah, based on the Shafi'i school. Find it on Google Play.

This app is a general guide. It does not replace the advice of a qualified scholar.
```
