Privacy Policy — Hajj Proximity

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Hajj Proximity alerts pilgrims when they approach a Meeqat boundary or enter/exit the Haram sanctuary around Makkah. It comes in two versions: Hajj Proximity (free) and Hajj Proximity Premium (a paid app that adds a Fidyah Calculator). This policy applies to both, and explains what data the apps use and why.

In short: the apps never send your location to any server of ours — there is no account, no server, and no backend. Everything the apps work out stays on your phone. The only outside service involved in showing the map is Google Maps (see "Maps" below).

Location data

The app uses your device's GPS location (including in the background, when tracking is enabled) to calculate your distance to the five fixed Meeqat points and whether you are inside the Haram boundary. This calculation happens entirely on your device.

  • The apps never store your raw location, and never send it to any server of ours or to any third party.
  • Only small derived flags are saved locally on your device (in app storage, not on a server): which Meeqat points you've already been alerted for, whether you were last inside or outside the Haram, and your chosen alert distance. This lets the app avoid repeating an alert you've already received.
  • Background location is used only to trigger Meeqat/Haram notifications while the app is closed. You can disable background tracking at any time from the Settings tab.

Other data stored on your device

Your Ihram checklist progress, alert settings (distance threshold, which alert types are enabled), your language choice, and the alert flags described above are saved locally on your device only, so they persist between app sessions. None of this is sent anywhere. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Fidyah Calculator (Hajj Proximity Premium)

The calculator works entirely on your device. The actions you select are used only to show the result on screen; they are not saved, not sent anywhere, and are cleared when you leave the calculator or close the app. In the free app, the Fidyah tab only shows a description and a button that opens the premium app's page on Google Play.

Fulfil Fidyah tab (Hajj Proximity Premium)

If you add a fidyah to the Fulfil tab, from the calculator or by hand, the fidyah you added and your progress on it are saved locally on your device only, so they persist between app sessions. They are never sent anywhere. You can remove any entry at any time, and deleting the app removes all of it.

Purchases

Hajj Proximity Premium is a paid app bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment; the app never sees or stores your payment details, and there are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Maps

The map screens use the Google Maps SDK to draw the map. To do this, Google loads map data over the internet, and Google may receive technical information such as your device's IP address and the map area you are viewing, the same way any map service does. How Google handles that is described in Google's Privacy Policy. Our apps do not send your location coordinates to Google or to anyone else.

Crash reporting

The app may use Sentry to automatically report crashes and errors, so problems can be fixed. If enabled, this sends technical diagnostic data (such as a stack trace, app version, and general device information) to Sentry. This data is used only for fixing bugs and is not linked to your identity or location.

What we don't do

  • No user accounts or sign-in
  • No advertising or ad tracking
  • No analytics that identify individual users
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties for marketing
  • No access to contacts, camera, microphone, or photos

Permissions the app requests

  • Location (foreground and background): required for proximity alerts, as described above.
  • Foreground service (location): lets the app keep checking your location in the background while tracking is turned on.
  • Notifications: required to alert you when you approach a Meeqat or cross the Haram boundary.
  • Internet access: required to load the map.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
